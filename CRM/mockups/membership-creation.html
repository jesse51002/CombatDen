Membership creation, redesigned

The admin panel's start-membership wizard, rebuilt in the kiosk signup flow's visual and interaction language. Same tokens, same composition — at admin scale, in a 1100 × 0.88vh dialog. Every feature of the current wizard survives; the separate discounts step does not.

A · The flow

The arithmetic. Today: payer → members → (plans → discounts) × N → review → [signWaivers] → preview → payment → results = 6 + 2N steps. One member is 8 screens; three is 12. Proposed: who → plans × N → [waivers] → review & charges → payment → results = 5 + N. Three members drop from 12 screens to 8.

Carried, without a frame of its own. The rail replaces the 3–4 group progress bar and its per-substep mini-bars — the repeat is expressed as Person 2 of 3 under the title and Step 3 of 8 in the topbar, so it can't lie the way today's hardcoded substepCount: 5 does against a real 2N + 3. Each rung keeps its aria-label (Step 2, Plans, person 2 of 3, current step) and the live one carries aria-current="step". Launch is unchanged: the button lives on a member's detail page, is blocked for a frozen member with Unfreeze this member before adding a membership, and entering as the tail of Add Member prepends an always-done Member added rung. Any previous run's breakdown is cleared when the dialog opens.

1 · Who's joining. The old payer and members steps, merged. One roster: each row carries its own getting a membership tick, its role pills and its row actions, and who pays is a control on this screen rather than a screen of its own. That kills the current flow's worst confusion — two adder pairs with identical copy pointing in opposite directions (add a payer / add a payee). Here there is one adder pair, and the payer is changed by a named button. Survives from today: the launch member is pre-ticked, a person added or linked in-flow is auto-added to the run, and entering as the tail of Add Member prepends an always-done Member added rung to the rail. Fixed, not inherited: changing the payer now says it clears the run, and unticking someone says what it discards.

Coverage

Everything else in the current wizard, and where it went. Nothing was dropped; a handful of capabilities are carried by a surface that has no frame of its own above.

Change who's paying opens a compact dialog identical in shape to Find an existing member (frame 13): choose between the launch member (Member getting membership) and any authorized payer (Authorized payer), read what the choice means before making it, or create or link a person and authorize them as the payer — a just-added payer is auto-selected. It is the same two adders as frame 1's, pointed the other way, which is precisely why they no longer sit side by side on one screen. Switching payer resets the run to the launch member; frame 1 says so before the dialog opens.

Failure twins. Couldn't load plans. Please try again. reuses C1's shape with a retry; a failed authorize in frame 14 replaces its green outcome notice with the named error in warm; Nothing to charge for this request. replaces the money panel's hero with $0.00 and the line Nothing to charge — these all start today at no cost., and the primary still commits.

Three deliberate changes to how a surviving capability behaves. (1) The preset grid became a list — a grid of value chips wastes the panel's width and buries the lifetime, which is the field that decides whether a discount is safe to apply. (2) The discount step was skippable; discounts are now simply optional — a membership card with no chips is a membership with no discounts, and there is no step left to skip. (3) The one-off card is blocked, not hidden, on a recurring or cash run (frame 8), where today it silently vanishes and any captured card is quietly ignored. Also unchanged in rule but restated on screen: Review's primary is disabled once every membership has been removed, with Nothing left to start — pick a plan for someone, or start over.
