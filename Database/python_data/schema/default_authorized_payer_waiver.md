# Authorized Payer Agreement

_This is a placeholder default. Each gym owns and may edit its own copy; edits
are versioned and the document can never be deleted._

By signing below, I (the "Authorized Payer") agree to be added as an authorized
payer for the member named at signing and acknowledge the following:

1. **Payment authorization.** I authorize this gym to bill my payment method on
   file for the memberships I am assigned to pay for. I understand that being an
   authorized payer does not, by itself, charge me — each membership separately
   records who pays it.

2. **Responsibility.** I accept responsibility for the recurring and one-time
   charges associated with the memberships I agree to pay for, until those
   memberships are cancelled or I am removed as an authorized payer.

3. **Changes and cancellation.** I understand I may be removed as an authorized
   payer at any time, and that removing me does not cancel the underlying
   memberships or refund charges already incurred.

4. **Accuracy.** The payment and contact information I have provided is accurate,
   and I will keep it current while I remain an authorized payer.

5. **Acknowledgment.** I have read and understood this agreement and am signing
   it voluntarily.
