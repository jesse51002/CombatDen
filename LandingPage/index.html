App that keeps members from quitting.

CombatDen is a member retention app for class-based gyms. Keep members engaged with content and rewards they care about, in an app built for your gym. Works alongside your current software, no payment migration required.

Your brand, everywhere

The app immerses members in your brand's vibe, imagery, and colors. Choose from 75+ ready-made designs, or we build one custom at no extra charge.

An agentic video feed for your gym

Create a video feed with our agent, tell it what you want and don't want, add your own videos, and it learns from what you remove. Every gym gets a different feed because every gym has a different brief.

Perfectly timed content

We engage members when it matters. Book a class and the right warm-up shows up 30 minutes before, so members stay longer.

A loyalty program that pays for itself

Members earn points for attending class and redeem them for rewards you configure, branded shirts, guest passes, discounted private training. Every reward is structured to drive attendance and grow long-term revenue.

Why retention matters

It costs roughly 5x more to find a new member than to keep one. Keeping just 5% more members in a 100-member gym is worth about $9,000 a year.
