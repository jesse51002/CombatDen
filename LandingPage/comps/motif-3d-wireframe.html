Shape candidates, inflated

Everything here is at balloon thickness, and everything is built from closed tubes only. The single-path forms are one loop bent and swept fat. The ball and globe are several loops sharing a centre, passing through each other rather than joining.

Ball

Three great circles

A sphere implied by the fewest loops that can imply one. Inflated they nearly touch, so it reads as a solid ball with slots cut into it.

Globe

Circles plus latitudes

Two horizontal rings added, so it reads as a globe rather than an orbit. Denser, and the extra rings cost the silhouette some clarity.

One stroke

Soft cube

A cube traced in a single unbroken path. Inflated this far the corners nearly meet, so it reads as a knot of eight lobes rather than a cube.

Trefoil

Overlapping loop

Passes through itself three times and fuses into one lumpy body at this thickness. The most alive of the set.

Rounded triangle

Bent wire

Three fat corners, three short runs. Holds its silhouette better than the others when inflated, because it has so few turns.

Ring

Plain loop

A doughnut once inflated. The hole is the only feature, so everything depends on keeping it open.

Coil

Wound loop

Six wraps around a ring. At balloon thickness the wraps merge and it turns into a ribbed torus, which is either a nice surprise or a lost shape.

Wander

Loose loop

No symmetry, just a closed path drifting through space. Friendliest and least engineered of everything here.

Reference

Soft cube, not inflated

The previous thickness, kept here so the jump to balloon is visible side by side rather than remembered.

Thickness

Same soft cube four ways. This dial decides whether it reads as wire, tube, or balloon.

Wire

Thin. Still a drawing.

Tube

Reads as an object.

Fat

Inflated. Corners swallow the runs.

Balloon

Form collapses into mass.
